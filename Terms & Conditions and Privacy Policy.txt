Privacy Policy
1. Overview

Voicely is an offline-first text-to-speech application that does not require user registration, login, or account creation. We respect user privacy and aim to collect minimal to no personal data.

2. Data Collection

Voicely does not directly collect, store, or process personal user data such as:

Name

Email address

Phone number

Location

Login credentials

3. Offline Usage

All core features of Voicely work completely offline.

When offline, the app displays only default local content.

4. Online Features & Banner Content

When the user is connected to the internet:

The app may download banner images and text content to the device.

These banners may include:

Informational messages (e.g., greetings, announcements)

Custom promotional or clickable content that may redirect to an external URL

Downloaded banner content is stored locally on the device for display purposes.

5. Advertisements

Voicely does not use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or similar ad networks.

The app may display customized third-party promotional content within the banner area when the user is online.

Clicking such content may open external websites, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Donations

Voicely provides an optional donation QR code for users who wish to support the app voluntarily.

Donations are processed via third-party UPI applications (such as Google Pay).

Voicely does not store or track transaction details.

Transaction records are handled entirely by the respective UPI app and financial institutions.

If a user voluntarily adds a note (for example, requesting contact for promotions), that information may be used solely for the stated purpose.

7. Data Storage

Banner images and related text may be cached locally on the device.

No cloud database is maintained by Voicely for user data.

8. Children’s Privacy

Voicely is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app under Settings, where the latest version will always be accessible.
